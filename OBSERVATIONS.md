# Real study-session observations

Session 1 records my feedback after using the app. Session 2 is left blank for a future session.

## Session 1

### Date and study context

I used What Next during a study session.

### What I tried

I tried using the app while studying and noticed two things I would like it to support: keeping track of study time and noting things to look up later.

### Where I drifted

Sometimes something came to mind that I wanted to search for after finishing my current task. I would like somewhere to put that thought for later.

### What felt cumbersome

The app does not show how long I have been studying. I want to track that time and be able to pause when I step away, such as for a toilet break or a scrolling break.

There is also no dedicated place to jot down things I want to look up after I finish.

### What might need changing

- Add a study timer with pause and resume, so breaks do not count as study time. I would also like to see how long I have studied continuously.
- Seeing something like “I studied for 30 minutes straight” might give me a sense of accomplishment and motivate me to continue. This is something I want to try, not a measured result from this session.
- Add a section for thoughts or things to search for later, so I can capture them and return to them after my current task.
